Open qualitative research with Taguette

For the Open qualitative research with Taguette workshop we will be using a free and open-source qualitative data analysis (QDA) tool called Taguette. Taguette helps researchers organize, annotate, and code textual documents. The setup instuctions and the data for this episode can be found here.

Open qualitative research with QualCoder

For the Open qualitative research with QualCoder workshop we will be using a free and open-source qualitative data analysis (QDA) tool called QualCoder. QualCoder helps organize, code, and analyze unstructured information such as text documents, images, audio, and video. The setup instuctions and the data for this episode can be found here.
