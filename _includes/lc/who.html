The course is aimed at anyone who works with or are planning to work with qualitative data. It is free and open to the public. You don't need to have any previous knowledge of the tools that will be presented at the workshop.

A series of Carpentries workshops put on by members of the Northeast Carpentries Community happening Wednesday afternoons in October that aims to teach participants basic concepts, skills, and two tools for working with qualitative data: Taguette and QualCoder.

Introduction to Working with Qualitative Data: This introductory workshop provides an overview of qualitative methodology and data interpretation. Participants will explore core practices for gathering non-numerical data (such as semi-structured interviews and focus groups) and practical techniques for thematic analysis and coding.
