Introduction to working with qualitative data

This introductory workshop provides an overview of qualitative methodology and data interpretation. Participants will explore core practices for gathering non-numerical data (such as semi-structured interviews and focus groups) and practical techniques for thematic analysis and coding.

October 14
Open qualitative research with Taguette

In this introduction to Taguette workshop, learners will practice working with secondary qualitative data from the Qualitative Data Repository (QDR) in the free open-source qualitative data analysis (QDA) tool Taguette. We will annotate and analyze interview transcripts from a qualitative study and look at options for sharing both source data and coded analysis projects.

Open qualitative research with QualCoder

Learn to use QualCoder, a free and open-source qualitative coding and data analysis tool. This workshop will provide introductory training in the principles of deductive and inductive qualitative coding through practice with QualCoder.
